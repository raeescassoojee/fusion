Where the AI actually runs

01

Risk model

Frequency, severity, recency and peak-time components score every geofence from five years of Discovery claims fused with SAPS context.

02

On-camera vision

COCO-SSD object detection and BlazeFace face detection run in the browser on live doorbell feeds — person, vehicle and face boxes at frame rate.

03

Server pipeline

Uploaded footage runs the full Python stack: YuNet faces, licence-plate detection with OCR, vehicle colour and type, quality-weighted trust scoring.

04

Evidence matching

Multi-signal comparison across cameras with a journey-plausibility filter produces a scored, explainable candidate link.

05

Optimisation

Risk-weighted routing maximises protected risk per kilometre, and every review outcome tunes the thresholds.

      recurring evidence · anonymous, human-reviewed

      Signatures that plausibly recur are clustered into patterns. A pattern is not a person: no name, no ID, no face gallery. It records what cameras observed and carries a SAPS reference outward — identity attribution stays with SAPS under their authority.
