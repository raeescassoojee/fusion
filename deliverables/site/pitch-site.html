POPIA
Governance is the feature

Minimum necessary,
provable always

P—01

Blur by default

Faces and plates are masked on every map and list view. Un-blurred evidence exists only inside an authorised case — and every open is logged.

P—02

Edge-first video

Routine footage never leaves the camera. Only event crops with checksums are uploaded, and temporary signatures expire on a 72-hour TTL.

P—03

No inferred identity

The system never infers race, gender, age, emotion or criminal intent — and it never auto-dispatches from similarity alone. Ever.

P—04

Evidence Passport

Source, checksum, model provenance, every transformation and every reviewer — a chain of custody POPIA and a court can both read.
